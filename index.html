About Glam by Khushali

Glam by Khushali is a beauty studio dedicated to personalized luxury makeup, professional skincare, and beautiful henna artistry. Every service is tailored to enhance your natural features, so you leave feeling confident, radiant, and completely yourself.

From timeless bridal looks to occasion-ready glam, each appointment begins with understanding your style, skin, and vision. Whether you are preparing for your wedding day or a special event, Glam by Khushali brings careful attention, artistry, and a polished finish to every detail.
